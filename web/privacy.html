JumpHeight Sync privacy

JumpHeight Sync is a small Mac app for riders testing the JumpHeight puck. When the puck is plugged in, the app copies the ride recording off it and uploads it to a folder it creates in your own Google Drive, and shares that folder with the JumpHeight developer so the ride can be analysed.

The app asks Google only for permission to manage files it creates itself. It cannot see, read, or change anything else in your Drive.

The app stores your Google sign-in token on your own Mac and nowhere else. It does not send your data to any server of ours; there is no server. If you sign in to Garmin through the app, that token is likewise stored only on your Mac, and your password is never saved.

To stop everything, quit the app and remove it. To revoke its access to your Drive, visit your Google account's third-party access page.
