4 Empty the puck

Once the ride has been sent, empty the puck so it starts your next ride with a clean slate. This is the step that protects the next session — a full puck wipes its own recording the next time it is picked up. The button switches on by itself once the ride has been checked and sent.

Nothing here can lose the ride — it stays on the puck either way. But the puck only holds about five hours of riding, and once it is full the next ride wipes the old detail (your jump counts survive). So copy each ride off before you go out again.
