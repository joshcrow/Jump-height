1 Connect to the puck

Plug the puck into this computer with its USB cable — the one it charges on — and click Connect with the cable. Pick the entry that mentions XIAO, JumpHeight or usbmodem. (The first time, the Mac may ask to allow the accessory — click Allow.)

Or, with the puck switched on and next to you, tap Connect over Bluetooth and pick the one whose name starts with JumpHeight.

If anything here goes wrong, nothing is lost — the puck keeps everything until step 4, and step 4 only appears once the ride has been copied in full and handed off.
