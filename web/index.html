JumpHeight

This site has one job: getting a ride off the puck and back to Josh, from a phone, with nothing to install.

Send a ride →

Everything happens on your phone. Nothing is uploaded anywhere unless you tap Send and choose where it goes.

Easiest with the USB cable: plug the puck into a computer and open this in Chrome. Over Bluetooth it also works from Chrome on Android or a computer, or from the free Bluefy app on an iPhone.
